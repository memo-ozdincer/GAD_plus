## Larger failure-taxonomy cohort

Job `1946008` used 24 deterministic random test-set samples (excluding the
three exploratory samples), a per-Cartesian-component Gaussian sigma of
0.25 A on the **labelled TS** with seed 42, standard projected GAD,
`dt=0.003`, and 300 steps.  This produced initial per-atom RMS displacements
of 0.358--0.511 A (mean 0.434 A), not a reactant/product midpoint. It packed twelve
independent 16-core g-xTB ranks on one 192-core CPU node.  The per-step
records and derived report are at:

`/scratch/memoozd/gadplus/runs/gxtb_gad_taxonomy_1946008/`

| final trajectory class | count |
|---|---:|
| index-1 force-limited | 9 |
| minimum-like (n_neg = 0) | 12 |
| multi-negative (n_neg > 1) | 3 |
| strict TS convergence | 0 |

The logged mode-continuity means were 0.995--0.997 across these three classes.
Thus the evidence does not identify ordinary lowest-mode tracking failure as
the primary cause.  Instead, initial noisy structures typically have many
negative vibrational eigenvalues; `lambda_2` passes near zero and then the
trajectory either becomes minimum-like or retains extra negative modes.

The report contains `lambda_2` initial/final/minimum magnitude, sign changes,
time at index 1, force minima, mode-index changes, displacement, and distance
to the labelled TS.  It is generated by
`scripts/analyze_gad_trajectories.py`.

## Matched Sella baseline

Job `1947512` is the matched 24-sample Sella baseline: same labels, noise,
seed, and g-xTB executable.  It uses `diag_every=1` and refreshes the supplied
direct Hessian after PES kicks.  It produced 22/24 strict TS convergences
(`n_neg == 1` and `fmax < 0.01 eV/A`) in 8--41 Sella steps.  The two remaining
rows are **calculator failures**, not scientifically valid failed optimizations:
sample 264 had g-xTB SCF non-convergence during a Hessian calculation and
sample 286 wrote a malformed 1294-value Hessian where 1296 values were
required.  They must not be counted as Sella successes or as GAD-vs-Sella
comparisons.

## Next diagnostic

Job `1947556` performed that direct GAD-only rescue on all 12 minimum-like
endpoints.  Each endpoint received a `+/- 0.05 A` Cartesian-RMS displacement
along its softest projected vibrational mode (mass weighting removed for the
Cartesian displacement), followed by 500 standard projected-GAD steps at
`dt=0.003`.

The result was 0/24 strict recoveries.  Twenty-two restarts ended again at
`n_neg=0`.  The positive sign did recover `n_neg=1` for samples 206 and 227,
but their final forces were respectively 0.119 and 0.154 eV/A, so neither is
a TS-gate pass.  Therefore a single soft-mode endpoint nudge is insufficient;
the result is still useful evidence that the minimum-like class can retain a
one-sided route back toward the index-1 basin.

## Tight-cap single-mode control

Job `1948510` repeated the 24-sample cohort using standard single-mode GAD
with `dt=0.001`, a 0.05 A maximum per-atom step, and 500 steps.  It had 0/24
strict passes.  Relative to the baseline, minimum-like endpoints decreased
from 12 to 7, index-1 force-limited rows remained 9, and multi-negative rows
increased from 3 to 8.  Thus a tight cap reduces collapse to minima but, at
this fixed step budget, sacrifices force progress and leaves extra unstable
modes unresolved.

Increasing the endpoint soft-mode kick from 0.05 to 0.15 A (job `1950884`)
increased index-1 recovery from 2/24 to 5/24, all on the same positive sign.
The best recovered branch (sample 84) ended at `n_neg=1`, `fmax=0.0714 eV/A`;
none passed the strict force gate within 500 steps.  This is evidence for a
one-sided escape route from the minimum-like endpoints, but not yet a
converged GAD rescue.

Job `1950888` continued all five recovered positive-kick branches with
standard single-mode GAD at `dt=0.003`.  All five strictly converged: sample
206 at step 1,354, sample 227 at 1,741, sample 84 at 2,189, sample 112 at
3,457, and sample 274 at 4,668.  Every endpoint had `n_neg=1` and
`fmax < 0.01 eV/A`.  A deliberately slower control for sample 84 at
`dt=0.001` remained `n_neg=1` but reached only `fmax=0.0185 eV/A` after 5,000
steps; it is not a convergence.
